# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no framework. Hosting planned on Netlify, domain opti-maintenance.fr (email already live on that domain: never touch MX records).

## Users

Two audiences in equal measure:
- Maintenance managers and site directors in industrial plants (agroalimentaire, manufacturing) facing a breakdown or needing reinforcement, often on a phone, sometimes urgently.
- Operators in the environment sector: water treatment (STEP), méthanisation units (often farmer-operators), collectivités, looking for a local technician.

## Product Purpose

One-page showcase site for Opti'Maintenance Industrie, the business of independent technician Malo Maheas, based in Mayenne (53). Success = a visitor understands within seconds what he repairs and where, then either calls him directly (urgent breakdown) or sends a written request (planned work / quote).

## Positioning

A single independent technician, not a company: one interlocutor who personally covers mechanics, electrical, variable-speed drives and automation, across both classic industry and environment installations (eau, méthanisation). Local, reachable, hands-on.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from Facebook, LinkedIn, word of mouth or a local search, mostly on mobile. Urgent cases want the phone number immediately; planned cases want to describe a need in writing.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Four service domains (wording from the client's own poster):
- Mécanique: entretien, réparation et maintenance des équipements industriels.
- Électrique: diagnostic, maintenance et mise en conformité des installations électriques.
- Variation de vitesse: installation, paramétrage et dépannage de variateurs de vitesse.
- Automatisme: diagnostic, modification et optimisation des automatismes et programmes.

Sectors: traitement de l'eau, méthanisation, toutes industries (agroalimentaire, industrie manufacturière, collectivités, etc.).

Undecided: exact intervention radius, years of experience / career history, SIRET and legal form, client consent for reusing Facebook photos.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Opti'Maintenance Industrie. Logo: gear wheel drawn with circuit traces, green on black, "INDUSTRIE" set in widely tracked capitals.
- Tagline: « La maintenance industrielle, simplement efficace. »
- Voice: first person singular (« j'interviens »). Never a fake corporate « nous ».
- Values on the poster: Fiabilité (interventions soignées et durables), Réactivité (délais courts, service efficace), Proximité (basé en Mayenne, proche de vos besoins). Promise: suivi personnalisé, grande réactivité, engagement total, un seul interlocuteur.

## Product Principles

1. Honest scale: an independent technician, shown as such. No inflated numbers, no corporate voice.
2. Field, not start-up: the register of a technician on site, not a tech product (no neon, no gadget effects).
3. Phone first for emergencies, written request for planned work; both always within reach.
4. Concrete over generic: name the equipment and the tasks, never « solutions innovantes ».
5. Both worlds equal: industry and environment carry the same weight.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast; usable one-handed on a phone in a plant or outdoors.
