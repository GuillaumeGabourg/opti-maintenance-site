# Surface: index.html (one-page showcase)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: industrial maintenance managers and environment operators (eau, méthanisation), equal weight, mostly mobile. Actions: call 06 87 61 97 39 (urgent) or send a written request (planned). Proof available: the client's own service wording, sectors, values; no testimonials or figures exist, none may be invented. Open: radius, experience, SIRET, photo consent.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the synoptic view of the visitor's own line: each lit piece of equipment is a domain Malo covers. Refuses the category default of photo hero + four icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: High-performance HMI language on graphite #16191b; equipment drawn as flat outlined faceplates, pipes and cables as strokes; green #8cc63f means "running / my domain" only; amber #e3a33b only for urgency. Archivo condensed caps for labels and display, JetBrains Mono only for measured values and tags. 1px seams, no shadows, 4px radii. Raises: locked palette (one-bit), single state vocabulary arrêt/marche/défaut/intervention (chromatophore), hairline seams + isolated emergency call (console), mono only for measurement (phosphor), fixed nameplate per domain (brine).

STORY: Visitor recognises their installation, sees that one independent technician covers mechanics, electrical, drives and automation across industry and environment, then calls or writes.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 1/3: tagline in condensed caps, first-person lede, primary call button, secondary planned-request button. Right 2/3: interactive synoptic (TGBT, API, variateur, moteur-pompe, cuve, digesteur); hovering/tapping an equipment opens its faceplate. Bottom: alarm strip with phone number.

FORM: Synoptique de supervision, position 7 on the ordered grounded list, seed key 41f50440.

Signature interaction: equipment faceplates selectable by hover/tap/keyboard, flow animation on pipes (disabled under reduced motion); the "domaines" section repeats the faceplates as nameplates.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
